How margin is calculated

Margin % = profit ÷ cost.

  • Cost ₹100, sold at ₹200 → profit ₹100 → 100% margin.
  • Cost ₹100, sold at ₹150 → profit ₹50 → 50% margin.
  • Selling below cost gives a negative margin (a loss).

Costs vs charges

  • Line items: your costs (item, print/unit, setup, shipping) and your selling price per unit.
  • All-in cost/unit spreads setup and shipping over the quantity — it is your break-even price.
  • Billed to client (setup fees, shipping, discount) changes revenue, not cost.
  • Job costs (other costs, payment gateway fee) are costs that aren’t on a line item.

Set a target margin. The calculator works out the price you need — including discount, GST and the payment fee — and suggests unit prices (rounded up) that reach it. Apply one row or all at once.
